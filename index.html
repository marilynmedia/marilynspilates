About Marilyn

Teaching Pilates the way it changed me.

I hold dual Mat and Reformer certifications through Club Pilates, and I'm currently completing an advanced Pilates teacher training program to deepen my practice even further — from special-population programming to more nuanced, client-specific instruction.

I believe good instruction meets people where they actually are, not where a standard class assumes they should be. That's the philosophy I bring to every session, whether it's a private client or a free community pop-up.
